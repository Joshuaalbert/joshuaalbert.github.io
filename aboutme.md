Hi there! My name is Josh Albert and I am the co-founder of Touch Medical Intelligence, a company that uses artificial intelligence to improve long-term health outcomes. I have always been interested in the intersection of different fields, and my background in math, physics, biology, and computation has given me a unique perspective on my work.

I believe that the benefits of academia and entrepreneurship can be combined to create positive change for humanity. As a PhD in astronomy, I have used my expertise in Bayesian methods to study a variety of topics, including the cosmic web and Earth's ionosphere. I am also the creator of the open-source Bayesian programming package JAXNS, which has gained recognition for promoting high-integrity open science.

In addition to my work with Touch Medical Intelligence, I also consult part-time for Caltech's Radio Camera Initiative, where I use my diverse expertise and innovative thinking to tackle some of the biggest questions in astronomy and astrophysics. My interests include radio interferometry, Bayesian probabilistic programming, cosmic dawn, basal cognition, computation, and emergent complexity theory. If you want to stay updated on my latest projects and insights, you can follow me on Twitter at @RealJoshAlbert.
